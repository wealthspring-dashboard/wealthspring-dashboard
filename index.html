Performance Thresholds

Each target below controls the green/yellow/red indicator on its matching card — hitting or beating it shows green, falling meaningfully short shows red. These aren't fixed rules, they're your own goals; adjust anytime.

Colors Monthly Revenue on Overview. No universal benchmark — set this to your own monthly goal.
Colors Net Profit Margin on Overview. For professional services firms, 15–25% net margin is commonly considered healthy; below ~10% is often a warning sign.
Colors Client Retention on Overview and Client Intelligence (trailing 12-month basis). Service firms often aim for 90%+; below 80% typically signals a real churn problem worth investigating.
Colors Billable Hours % on Team & Operations. Bookkeeping/accounting firms commonly target 65–80% — lower for owners/managers who also handle sales and admin, higher for dedicated staff.
Colors Cash Balance on Overview. A common small-business guideline is 3–6 months of operating expenses held in reserve — set this to whatever that works out to for you.
Colors At-Risk Clients on Overview red once the count reaches this number. Default of 1 means any past-due client shows red immediately — raise it if a client or two carrying a balance is normal for how you bill.
Colors Top-3 Client Concentration on Client Intelligence red once your top 3 clients account for this share of revenue. Many small businesses treat 40–60%+ concentrated in a handful of clients as a real diversification risk.

Integrations
Need Help?

Integration guides below. Contact us for support.

QuickBooks Online

Sync P&L, Balance Sheet, and customer revenue data in real-time.

1. Go to developer.intuit.com and create a new app
2. Copy your Client ID and Client Secret
3. Set redirect URI: https://wealthspring-dashboard.vercel.app/api/auth/qbo/callback
4. Paste credentials in Integrations section above
QuickBooks Time

Sync team hours for billable-hours tracking, effective cost-per-hour, and per-person revenue attribution.

1. Go to the QuickBooks Time API Add-On page and create an application
2. Set the OAuth Redirect URI to: https://wealthspring-dashboard.vercel.app/api/auth/qbtime/callback
3. Toggle QuickBooks Time on above and sign in when prompted
